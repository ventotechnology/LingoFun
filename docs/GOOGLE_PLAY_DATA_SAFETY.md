# LingoFun: Learn & Play — Google Play Data Safety Declarations

This document specifies the exact questionnaire responses required for the **Data safety** section in the Google Play Console for **LingoFun** (`com.antigravity.lingo_fun`).

---

## 1. Data Collection & Sharing Overview

| Question | Response | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** | **Yes** | User account identifiers for authentication and profile sync. |
| **Is all user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?** | **Yes** | All data is transferred over secure HTTPS / TLS 1.3 to Firebase servers. |
| **Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted?** | **Yes** | Users can delete account and profile directly from within the app settings or via email request. |

---

## 2. Data Types Declared

### A. Personal Info
- **Name**: Collected for app functionality (user profile display). Not shared with third parties. Ephemeral/Account-linked.
- **Email Address**: Collected for authentication and account recovery via Firebase Authentication. Not shared with third parties.
- **User IDs**: Account UID generated by Firebase Authentication. Used strictly for app functionality and data sync.

### B. App Activity
- **App Interactions**: User progress, lesson completions, quiz scores, XP, and streak counts. Stored to track educational progress. Not shared with any third party.

---

## 3. Advertising & Tracking
- **Does your app contain advertising?**: **No**
- **Does your app collect data for advertising or marketing purposes?**: **No**
- **Zero Third-Party Trackers**: No third-party ad networks, tracking SDKs, or data brokers are integrated.
