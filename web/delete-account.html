🦜 LingoFunLearn — Account & Data Deletion Request

At LingoFunLearn (published by Walletmix Limited), we respect your privacy and provide full control over your personal data and account records.

How to Delete Your Account:

You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated data through either of the methods below:

Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and profile directly from within the application at any time:

  • Open LingoFunLearn on your device.
  • Sign in to your account.
  • Navigate to the Profile tab.
  • Scroll to the bottom of the profile settings and tap "Delete Account".
  • Confirm your choice. Your account, progress, and authentication tokens are purged immediately from Firebase.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can submit an account deletion request via email. Please include the email address associated with your LingoFunLearn account:

What Data is Deleted:

  • User Profile: Your display name, email address, profile avatar, and Firebase authentication credentials.
  • Learning Progress: All lesson scores, quiz results, XP points, and streak counters.
  • Preferences: Target languages, native language settings, audio settings, and saved progress.

Data Retention Policy:

Upon receiving an account deletion request, all personal data is permanently wiped from our Firebase Authentication and database instances within 48 hours. No personal identifiable information is retained after deletion.
