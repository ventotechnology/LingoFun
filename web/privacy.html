Privacy Policy for LingoFun

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Welcome to LingoFun: Learn Languages with Fun & Play ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy describes our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your data when using our iOS, Android, and Web applications.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Authentication Information: When you sign up or log in using Google Sign-In or email, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo provided by Google OAuth or Firebase Authentication.
  • Learning & Gamification Progress: We store your language learning tracks, completed units, lesson XP, streak counts, gems, mascot wardrobe selections, and mistakes notebook entries to provide a seamless learning experience across devices.
  • Microphone & Speech Data: With your explicit consent, our speaking challenge feature uses your device microphone for interactive speech recognition and pronunciation feedback. Audio recordings are processed in real-time on your device and are never permanently stored or shared with third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate and manage your learner profile securely via Google and Firebase Auth.
  • To personalize your learning curriculum, track progress, and award badges.
  • To evaluate speaking exercises and provide instant feedback.
  • To maintain, protect, and improve our services.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We utilize industry-standard services solely for core application features:

  • Google Identity & Firebase Authentication: For secure authentication and credential handling.
  • Google Cloud & Firebase: For secure data storage and real-time backend synchronization.

4. Children's Privacy

LingoFun is designed for learners of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 without parental consent.

5. Account Deletion & Data Rights

You have the full right to access, update, or delete your account data at any time directly through the Profile tab in the application or by contacting us at support@lingofun.vpshub.biz.
